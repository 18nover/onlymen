# Product Spec — OnlyMen

**Last updated:** 2026-11-20  
**Status:** Living document — update in place, keep history in Decision Log when material changes occur.

---

## 1. Vision

OnlyMen is a cleaner, modern, fully-owned alternative to RentMen.men.  
We are not cloning or wrapping an existing product. We are building our own platform under the 18nover company on the domain **onlymen.gay**.

Goal: treat this like a real company from day one and see how far it can go.

---

## 3. Phase 1 Rules (Current)

- Free for everyone (platform owner + any models who join).
- No subscription fees.
- Optional tips only.
- Grow the platform and community first.
- Pricing / monetization decisions are deferred until the platform has demonstrated value.

---

## 4. Primary Users (Initial)

- **Models / Escorts** — Create profiles, manage availability, receive tips, control visibility.
- **Clients** — Browse, contact, tip.
- **Platform owner** — Full admin, moderation, growth.

(Detailed personas and flows will be expanded as we learn.)

---

## 5. Non-Negotiables (so far)

1. We own the product, brand, data, and infrastructure completely.
2. Phase 1 stays free + tips only.
3. Documentation remains chronological and living.
4. Prefer small working increments over large unshipped plans.

---

## 6. Out of Scope for Phase 1

- Paid subscriptions or membership tiers
- Complex payment processing beyond basic tipping
- Advanced matching / AI recommendation engines (can come later)
- Mobile native apps (web-first)
